A few files in this directory:

spanish_lemmas.conllu: a small batch of sentences to address https://github.com/stanfordnlp/stanza/issues/1255

es.future.conllu: includes future tense and some other constructions, from this issue: https://github.com/stanfordnlp/stanza/issues/1549

spanish_como_train.conllu, spanish_como_test.conllu: train and test of adding como as a VERB: https://github.com/stanfordnlp/stanza/issues/1440

spanish_vosotros_train.conllu, spanish_vosotros_test.conllu: 2nd person plural (vosotros, vosotras, vuestro, os and 2pl verbs), which GSD and AnCora barely cover: https://github.com/stanfordnlp/stanza/issues/1707
